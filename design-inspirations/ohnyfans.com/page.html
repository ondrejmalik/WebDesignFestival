Signature greeting meaning roughly “my guy” or “my bro”, usually accompanied by the hand sign. It eventually became the name of his 2024 CS2 project, DRILLAS.

THE $129K OPENING (Restricted)

July 2023 opening of approximately $129,000 in rare CS cases and capsules. It attracted a huge live audience and produced very little to justify the cost.

DRILLAS (Restricted)
