- Before you pay

Fair questions.

Do I pay before or after I submit?

After. You build your entire entry, review the rubric, and only pay at the final step. Drafts save as you type, so you can leave and come back.

What if I don’t win?

You still receive the complete criterion-by-criterion scorecard and your weighted score out of 10 - the same breakdown every winner gets. You just don’t get the award page or badge. On Premium and rushed, your permanent nominee page stays either way.

Is a paid submission more likely to win?

No. The rubric and the jury are identical for every entry. The fee buys your place in the queue and, on rushed, a faster turnaround - never a better score.

What’s the difference between $39.99 and $79.99?

Speed only. Rushed submissions jump the queue and get a guaranteed 48-hour verdict instead of the standard cycle. Same rubric, same judges.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If for any reason we can’t judge your submission, you get a full refund. Once a review is delivered the fee is non-refundable.

How do agency plans work?

They’re annual programs for studios that want ongoing visibility - verified profiles, ranking placement, included submissions, and buyer leads. Pick a tier above - you can upgrade anytime.
