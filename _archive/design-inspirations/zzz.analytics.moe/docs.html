What is "Free Training Mode"?

"Free Training Mode" is a specific Deadly Assault stage available in beta releases, used as a neutral testing ground for Agents.

The enemy utilised is a modified version of Dead End Butcher, having a notably increased HP pool, a linear Point Curve, no Resistances or Weaknesses, no Stage Buffs, and no Boss Buffs.
