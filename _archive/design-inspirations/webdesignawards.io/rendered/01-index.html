- 03 / What wins here

What wins in UI / UX Design.

The UI / UX Design category collects every ui / ux design site nominated for a Web Design Award and scores each one in public against the same 5 weighted criteria as every other category. Only the entries that clear the bar earn a permanent place in the archive.

The ranking above is by overall jury score, all-time - the sites that win here make their quality legible in the first few seconds. Looking for the studios behind the work? Browse the UI / UX Design agency directory.

01
Design & CraftThe visual system and the finish on it: typography, colour, composition, imagery and motion, down to details most visitors never consciously notice.
02
ExperienceWhether a first-time visitor understands what the site is and can do the main thing - judged on a phone first, then on a desktop.
03
OriginalityAn idea, interaction or way of telling the story that you haven't seen before - and that serves the visitor rather than getting in their way.
04
ContentCopy and media that make the case quickly and reward a closer read. Words count as much as pixels.
05
TechnicalMeasured, not judged: Google Lighthouse tests the site as a mid-range phone on a mobile connection, and the score is 60% its performance score and 40% its accessibility score.
