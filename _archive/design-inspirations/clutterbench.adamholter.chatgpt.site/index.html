How it works

The task

Each model, running as an agent, reads a blog post and builds one 1200 by 1200 graphic in HTML. It browses the publisher's site for fonts, colours and logo, and may not look at the post's own figures.

Two prompts

Raw says only "make a tasteful graphic to go with the post". Simple adds plain instructions to show one idea, once, with nothing extra. The gap between them is how much a model needs to be told.

The grader

Claude Haiku 5.5 labels every element twice. Where the two passes differ, Claude Sonnet 5.5 breaks the tie. The rules were written from one person's hand labels on 24 graphics.

Leaderboard: 588 graphics from 15 models at 49 model and reasoning effort combinations, 6 posts and 2 prompts, one attempt per cell. Each model is shown at its best reasoning effort, the one with the lowest clutter on the raw prompt in this same data, which flatters models that were run at more efforts. Clutter is the share of drawn area marked remove, plus half the area marked shrink. With one attempt per cell, gaps of a few points between models are not meaningful. The ClutterBench results post is shown but not scored, because it tells the model what clutter is. Updated Nov 12, 2026.
