How it works

The task

Each model, running as an agent, reads a blog post and builds one 8 second, 1280 by 720 animation in HTML. It may browse the publisher's site, the post included, for fonts, colours and logo, but the visual has to be its own.

Clutter over time

Every element is tracked through the animation. A mark sits on its element in each frame, so it moves and fades with it. The score is the clutter in each frame, averaged over the run.

The grader

The same two passes of Claude Haiku 5.5 as the still graphics, with Claude Sonnet 5.5 breaking ties. Its rules add motion: title cards, decorative movement and end cards that restate the chart are marked remove.

Leaderboard: 288 animations from 10 models at 24 model and reasoning effort combinations, 6 posts and 2 prompts, one attempt per cell. Each model is shown at its best reasoning effort, the one with the lowest clutter on the raw prompt in this same data, which flatters models that were run at more efforts. Clutter is the share of drawn area marked remove, plus half the area marked shrink, averaged over the 8 seconds. With one attempt per cell, gaps of a few points between models are not meaningful. The ClutterBench results post is shown but not scored, because it tells the model what clutter is. Beta: the animation grader has not been checked against hand labels yet, so read the numbers as rough. Updated Nov 10, 2026.
