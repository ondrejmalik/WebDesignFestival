Eventbrite Product Updates

Every month, we ship meaningful improvements to Eventbrite based on your feedback.Explore every feature we’ve released, check our product roadmap, or share your feedback

72 new features and improvements since April 2026

October 2026

12 LIVE UPGRADES THIS MONTH

Oct 30, 2026

Delay QR code release

Choose to release QR codes 24 or 48 hours before your event, making it harder to resell tickets in advance.

Oct 30, 2026

What’s next for Eventbrite

Explore our November 2026 roadmap: automated waitlists, SMS campaigns (paid, starting in the US), smarter event discovery and more, all coming in the months ahead.

Oct 19, 2026

Bulk check-in and check-out

Check in or out multiple attendees at once, with optional ticket-type filters, to save time before and after your event.

Oct 16, 2026

Clearer start and end date fields

Start and end dates now have their own fields when you create an event, so it's clearer how to set overnight and multi-day schedules.

Oct 14, 2026

Pin a featured event

Pin one event to the top of your organizer profile, so your flagship events are highlighted and not buried under smaller ones.

Oct 11, 2026

Attendee questions reach the right team

“Contact the organizer” messages go only to team members with permission for that profile. Admins and Owners can choose in Communication preferences.

Oct 6, 2026

Customizable attendee emails

Start with a fresh, modern design, then customize brand colors, light or dark mode, and background visuals. Preview your email on mobile and desktop before sending.

Oct 1, 2026

Reports by organizer profile

When creating a report, filter events by one or more Organizer Profiles. Matching events are grouped by profile, so you can easily create targeted reports.

WHAT ELSE IS ON

  1. Oct 21, 2026

    Duplicate add-ons in one click

    Copy any add-on, with price, capacity, sale dates and image already filled in.

  2. Oct 13, 2026

    Add-ons easier to find in checkout

    Buyers who only want add-ons can click Add-ons at the top of checkout, no ticket needed. Recurring events can sell add-ons on their own for the first time.

  3. Oct 12, 2026

    Paste formatted text into Email Campaigns

    Copy and paste text into your email body without losing formatting, so you can build campaigns faster without having to reformat everything manually.

  4. Oct 5, 2026

    Take card payments at the door on Android

    Staff using the Organizer app on Android can now enter card details manually for events in CAD, AUD, and GBP, just like on iOS.
