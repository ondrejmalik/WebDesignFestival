A Classic Reinvented

Three years ago, the Wooting 60HE changed the industry for good, but we've learned a lot since then, and we can do better. Now equipped with a more powerful chip, an aluminium shell, and packed with features that enhance both sound and feel, this is the Wooting 60HE v2.

Classic and Split-spacebar Layout

The new split-spacebar layout gives you extra control and versatility. It's something we're convinced you'll appreciate.
Or stick to the tried-and-true traditional layout. The OG your fingers already main.

The choice is yours. Pick wisely.

Aluminium Case

An upgraded design that stays true to the original, made of solid aluminium to give you that hefty premium feel.

ABS Case

An upgraded design that stays true to the original 60HE. Made of ABS plastic with a fixed strap mount and a unique aesthetic feature: a silicone 'wing' that accentuates the keyboard's speed and lift. Also includes EPDM dampening foam.

PBT Keycaps

The pre-built 60HE v2 comes with durable PBT keycaps. They have a slight texture for extra grip, and their legends are transparent, letting through the RGB light of your choice.

PBT keeps its texture, doesn't shine over time, and the legends won't fade.

Lekker Tikken Switch

The Lekker Tikken Switch is the latest addition to the Lekker family. It delivers highly stable key presses with a full 4mm travel range for optimal control, and its closed bottom gives your keystrokes a deeper, more muted sound than most other HE switches.

A true gamer's delight.

FR4 Switch Plate

The FR4 plate balances hardness with flex. The hardness removes any bounce from your keystrokes, increasing your accuracy in-game. It also gives you a soft, muted sound that's best described as woody and mellow.

You'll enjoy the soothing sound as you move through your game with high accuracy.

HD Poron® Sandwich

High-density Poron® foam sandwich that's placed between the PCB and switch plate, this layer dampens vibrations, and lowers the pitch for a deeper sound.

Friction Fit Pad

The friction fit pad holds your PCB securely without any screws, reducing vibrations and case resonance for a cleaner, deeper sound, while keeping your keystrokes smooth.

PET Film

Located directly under the switches, this acoustic layer dampens high-pitched sounds and shapes your keyboard's acoustics into a cleaner, marbly tone.

Gamers hear the pop, enthusiasts feel the depth.

Bottom Case Dampening

Change your keyboard's sound by swapping different dampening layers in the bottom case.(Exclusive to Aluminium case version)

Interchangeable Strap Mounts

Although the Wooting strap is an iconic piece that completes any Wooting build, we've added interchangeable strap and no-strap mounts so you can decide for yourself.

It all circles back to that ancient question: to strap or not to strap?
(Exclusive to Aluminium case version)

Why did we fire our keycap installer last year?

Performance, properly.

True 8kHz polling, with scanning locked in perfect sync. The result: 0.125ms input speed, on every key.

The power of analog

Instead of treating every key-press as simply on or off, your keyboard tracks the entire movement, unlocking faster input, finer control, and a growing range of gaming features.

Rapid Trigger

The feature Wooting pioneered that changed the peripheral landscape. It resets and activates keys the instant you move, for faster, more responsive input.

Adjustable Actuation

Fine-tune each key's actuation point with 0.1mm precision. Your keys respond at the exact depth you want them to.

The Wooting 60HE v2 will be available in both ANSI and ISO layouts, each offered with a regular or split spacebar option.

The ANSI Layout always comes pre-installed with US English double-shot PBT keycaps. The ISO comes without keycaps installed. The selected language keycap is a separate product you receive in addition.

We offer the following ISO keycap languages:
• UK English
• German
• Nordic (Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, and Danish)
• French
• Spanish (regular spacebar only)

If we don't offer your ISO layout language, you can choose the ISO model without keycaps and install any MX stem compatible keycaps with your language on it.

We will provide a black and bright silver colour for the pre-assembled models.

The Wooting 60HE v2 has hall effect sensors on the PCBA to detect the magnet inside the Lekker switch. Without the magnet it can't detect the switch. We recommend using our own Lekker switches. The prebuilt 60HE v2 comes with Lekker Tikken switches pre-installed.

Check out Jed's HE Switch Database for an extensive list of available switches.

When in doubt feel free to reach out to us on Discord.

Any keycaps compatible with Cherry MX switches should be compatible. For the bottom of ANSI and ISO row we use standard keycap sizes.

For the split spacebar layout, make sure to get the right caps for the spacebar area.


You can install almost any 3rd party keycap set on Lekker switches. If the keycap has the (Cherry) mx stem mount, it's compatible. If you have a Cherry profile keycap set, the Lekker switch also fits without a problem. If there's any obstruction, you can take out and install the Lekker switch south facing.

The split spacebar is from left to right a 2.75u, 1.25u, and a 2.25u key. These are common keys on an ANSI layout. It's the same as Left Shift, Ctrl, and Right Shift key.

Yes, the Lekker Tikken switches will be available in separate switch packs. It will also be available in Q4 2025.

Yes, the Lekker Tikken switches are compatible with all our Hall effect (HE) keyboards.

We will be monitoring the market response on Lekker Tikken to see if we should create more weights. We are confident that we chose a balanced weight between performance (light weight) and comfort (medium-high weight).

Yes, the 60HE v2 features per key configurable RGB effect, as well as special effects and RGB presets.

In our testing we haven't measured any performance issues or a significantly increased CPU load. (Mice that poll at 8k are constantly sending relative position data updates to the computer which is known to cause increased CPU load, whereas the data stream sent by keyboard polling is very different.). A PC should have no issues running the Wooting 60HE v2 at 8kHz. It is however possible to manually lower the polling rate of the keyboard.

The minimum required port is a USB 2.0 port. Anything higher like 3.x or 4.x is compatible as well.

Yes, through the Wootility you can set various polling speeds from 1kHz up to 8kHz.

Yes, the Wooting 60HE v2 PCBA is compatible with all GH60 standard cases, like the Tofu60 and Holy60 cases, if you remove the friction fit pad and mount the PCBA using screws.

Yes, the 60HE v2 is compatible with most aftermarket cases. Note that not all cases allow for a friction fit mount, but it's always possible to use the tray mount style and screw it in.

Yes, the Wooting 60HE v2 is compatible with most after market screw in stabilizers and clip in stabilizers.

No. It's not possible to switch between the regular and split spacebar, since the PCBAs are different.
