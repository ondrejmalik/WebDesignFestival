[ TRANSIT ROUTES ]

[ 01 // TRANSPORTATION TO KUTNA HORA ]

[ RAILWAY FROM PRAGUE ]

Direct regional and express trains depart hourly from Prague Main Station (Hlavní Nádraží) to Kutná Hora hl.n. Average transit duration: 52 minutes.

[ FESTIVAL SHUTTLE CONNECTION ]

Shuttle buses operate between Kutná Hora main railway station and the festival perimeter gate every 15 minutes during active festival hours (11:00–04:00).

[ ONSITE LOGISTICS ]

[ 02 // CAMPING VILLAGE & CASHLESS SYSTEM ]

[ TENT CAMPING ]

Open Thursday July 9 (12:00) through Monday July 13 (14:00). Features hot water sanitation, mobile showers, power recharging lockers, and drinking fountains.

[ 100% CASHLESS OPERATION ]

All concessions, beverage bars, and official merchandise kiosks accept NFC wristbands only. Top up online in advance or at physical onsite service booths.
