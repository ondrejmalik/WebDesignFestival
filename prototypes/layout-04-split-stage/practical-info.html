[ 🚆 Transit to Kutna Hora ]

[ Direct Trains from Prague ]

Direct express trains run hourly from Prague Main Station (Praha hl.n.) directly to Kutna Hora hl.n. Travel duration is approximately 50 minutes.

[ Tip: Reserve seat tickets in advance via České dráhy (ČD) app. ]

[ Free Festival Shuttle Bus ]

Dedicated festival shuttle buses run back and forth between Kutna Hora hl.n. train terminal and the Main Festival Gate every 15 minutes around the clock.

[ Transit time: 7 minutes • 100% free with festival ticket. ]

[ Car Parking & Carpooling ]

Guarded parking zone P1 is situated at the town perimeter. Follow festival road signage from Route 38. Capacity: 800 vehicles.

[ Fee: 100 CZK (~€4) per day • EV charging stalls available. ]

[ ⛺ Camping, Accommodation & Venue Services ]

[ Tent Village Camping ]

The festival tent grounds open on Friday, July 10 at 12:00 and remain open continuously until Monday, July 13 at 12:00.

Includes 24/7 security perimeter, drinking water stations, flush toilets, and hot shower facilities.

[ 100% Cashless RFID System ]

Festival of Darkness 2 operates a completely cashless environment. Your admission wristband contains an RFID microchip for all purchases.

Top-up online before the festival or at 6 designated top-up kiosks using payment card or cash (CZK/EUR).

[ Lockers & Safe Storage ]

Secure lockable storage boxes with internal USB-C charging sockets are available adjacent to the accreditation tent.

Pre-booking recommended via festival ticket portal.

[ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ]

[ Q: What is the festival policy regarding rain and adverse weather? ]

A: Festival of Darkness 2 runs rain or shine. In case of rainfall, the Lunar Forest Dome provides a 100% covered, climate-controlled indoor sanctuary with 1,800 capacity. Shows on both stages continue on schedule.

[ Q: Are children and pets allowed on festival grounds? ]

A: Children aged 12 and under enjoy free admission when accompanied by an adult parent or guardian. Hearing protection earmuffs are mandatory for minors. Pets are strictly prohibited due to high sound levels.

[ Q: Can I bring outside food and beverages? ]

A: Empty reusable water bottles (up to 1.0 liter) are allowed and can be refilled for free at all drinking water fountains. Outside glass bottles, cans, and commercial alcohol are prohibited.

[ Q: Where can I receive first aid or report lost items? ]

A: The Red Cross medical station and the Lost & Found service are located next to the Info Point at the Main Entrance, operational 24/7.
