[ Festival Stages & Venue Guide ]

[ Kutna Hora Festival Grounds | 2 Distinct Acoustic & Sensory Environments ]

[ The festival grounds in Kutna Hora are architecturally structured into two core performance spaces: the natural open-air amphitheater of Solar Horizon Stage and the climate-controlled indoor rain shelter of Lunar Forest Dome. ]

[ ⚡ Side-by-Side Stage Comparison ]

[ ☀️ Solar Horizon Stage ]

Open-Air

Architecture: Natural sloping grass amphitheater with panoramic sunset views.

Capacity: 4,500 visitors

Audio & Rigging: L-Acoustics K2 line array, high-throw stage lighting towers, open-sky fireworks display.

Musical Focus: Post-Rock crescendos, Indie Rock bands, Dream Pop, and energetic festival headliners.

Weather Factor: Open-air space with partial sunshade canopies. Bring sun protection during daytime hours.

[ 🌙 Lunar Forest Dome ]

Covered Rain Shelter

Architecture: Geodesic high-capacity tension marquee dome nestled inside the perimeter pine forest.

Capacity: 1,800 visitors

Audio & Rigging: Funktion-One point-source sound system, custom 360-degree laser mapping, immersive quadraphonic surround array.

Musical Focus: Hypnotic Dark Techno, Modular Ambient, IDM, Glitch, and late-night audiovisual club sessions.

Weather Factor: 100% weatherproof covered rain shelter. Features climate control and sealed wooden flooring for safe dry footing during sudden downpours.
