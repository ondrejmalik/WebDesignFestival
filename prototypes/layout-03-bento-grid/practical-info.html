[ 🚆 Transit to Kutna Hora ]

TRANSPORTATION

[ By Train from Prague / Brno ]

Direct České dráhy (ČD) trains depart from Prague Main Station (Praha hl.n.) every hour. Travel time is approximately 50 minutes to Kutná Hora hlavní nádraží.

[ Free Festival Shuttle Bus ]

Official festival shuttle buses run continuously between Kutná Hora train station and Main Gate 1 every 15 minutes. Free of charge with festival ticket or wristband.

[ Vehicle Parking (Lot P1) ]

Official parking lot P1 is situated at the town perimeter with 800 vehicle capacity. Flat rate fee of 100 CZK (~€4) per day. RV camper parking available in designated zone P2.

[ 🌧️ Rain Policy ]

WEATHER RESILIENCE

Festival Takes Place Rain or Shine!

In the event of heavy rainfall, outdoor operations remain supported while the indoor Lunar Forest Dome provides 100% dry shelter with capacity for 1,800 visitors.

[ 💳 100% Cashless RFID System ]

PAYMENTS

All beverage bars, food catering trucks, official merchandise booths, and lockers operate strictly using RFID wristbands. No cash or direct cards at vendor stalls.

[ Top-up Online or On-Site ]

Recharge your wristband online before the festival or at 3 physical top-up stations using cash (CZK/EUR) or payment card.

[ Free Balance Refunds ]

Any remaining wristband credit is 100% refundable without processing fees via our post-event portal within 14 days.

[ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Ground Regulations ]

FAQ & SAFETY

Q: Are children permitted at the festival?

A: Children up to 12 years of age enter free of charge when accompanied by a ticket-holding legal guardian. High-grade ear protection earmuffs are mandatory for children.

Q: Can I bring pets or animals?

A: Pets and animals are strictly prohibited from entering festival grounds and camping zones due to acoustic volume levels and safety regulations.

Q: What items are banned at security check?

A: Glass containers, outside alcoholic beverages, weapons, pyrotechnics, professional recording rigs without media accreditation, and laser pointers are strictly banned.
