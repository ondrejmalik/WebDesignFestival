[ Kutna Hora Festival Grounds | 2 Complementary Performance Environments ]

2 STAGES

[ The festival grounds in Kutna Hora are architecturally divided into two primary performance spaces: the open-air Solar Horizon amphitheater for energetic afternoon and evening sets, and the fully covered, weatherproof Lunar Forest Dome for immersive audiovisual and nocturnal electronic music. ]

[ ☀️ Solar Horizon Stage ]

OPEN-AIR MAIN STAGE
[ Wireframe Diagram: Open-Air Amphitheater Stage Layout ]

Type: Natural open-air hillside amphitheater

Capacity: 4,500 visitors

Acoustics: Line-array surround sound optimized for live band setups, dynamic guitar delays, and evening dance headliners

Musical Focus: Post-Rock, Indie Rock, Dream Pop, Synthwave

[ View Solar Stage Timetable → ]

[ 🌙 Lunar Forest Dome (Covered Rain Shelter) ]

SCENARIO 1: ALL-WEATHER SHELTER
[ Wireframe Diagram: Geodesic Marquee Dome & 360° Laser Trussing ]

Type: Heavy-duty reinforced marquee geodesic dome with complete rain & wind protection

Capacity: 1,800 visitors (dry, weather-sealed, dual-air-exchange ventilation)

Visuals: 360-degree projection mapping, spatial quadraphonic audio, laser array

Musical Focus: Dark Techno, Modular Ambient, Experimental IDM, Drone & Hypnotic Club Sets

[ Venue Map & Ground Services ]

GROUNDS ORIENTATION

[ Interactive Festival Grounds Map Scheme ]

Gate 1 (North Station Shuttle) ↔ Solar Horizon Stage (Amphitheater East) ↔ Lunar Forest Dome (Covered West) ↔ Tent Camping Village (South) ↔ Cashless RFID Central Hub ↔ Medical First Aid

[ ⛺ Tent Village Camping ]

Located on the southern slope 300 meters from stages. Quiet zone enforced 05:00 to 11:00.

[ 💳 Cashless Top-up Hubs ]

3 physical service booths near entrances and inside central plaza for RFID band management.

[ 🚑 First Aid & Water Points ]

24-hour Red Cross medical tent adjacent to Gate 1. Free potable tap water stations across all areas.
