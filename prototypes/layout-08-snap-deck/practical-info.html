[ 🚆 Transportation to Kutna Hora ]

[ By Train from Prague ]

Direct trains run hourly from Prague Main Station (Hlavní Nádraží) to Kutná Hora hl.n. (travel time approx. 52 minutes).

[ Festival Shuttle Bus ]

Dedicated festival shuttle buses connect Kutná Hora main railway station directly to the festival main gate every 15 minutes during peak hours (11:00–04:00).

[ ⛺ Camping & Cashless Wristbands ]

[ Tent Village ]

Open from Thursday July 9, 12:00 until Monday July 13, 14:00. Equipped with 24/7 warm showers, mobile restrooms, charging hubs, and safety lockers.

[ 100% Cashless Grounds ]

All bars, food trucks, and merch booths operate via NFC wristband. Top up online before the event or at physical top-up kiosks across the grounds.

[ 🌧️ Weather Preparedness & Rain Shelter ]

[ Kutna Hora festival grounds feature the indoor Lunar Forest Dome (1,800 capacity) providing complete storm and rain protection with non-stop programming. In case of severe weather, outdoor stage sets transition into the covered dome according to our backup schedule. ]
