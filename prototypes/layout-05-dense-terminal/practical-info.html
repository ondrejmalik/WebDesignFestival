[ Protocol 01: Transit & Rail Dispatches ]

[ 🚆 RAIL TRANSIT // PRAGUE → KUTNA HORA ]

Direct express trains depart hourly from Praha hl.n. (Prague Main Station) arriving at Kutna Hora hl.n. in approx. 50 minutes.

[ CARRIER: České dráhy (ČD) // Advance seat reservations recommended. ]

[ 🚌 SHUTTLE BUS DISPATCH // FREE OF CHARGE ]

Dedicated high-capacity festival shuttles run continuously between Kutna Hora hl.n. terminal and the Festival Main North Gate.

[ FREQUENCY: Every 15 min around the clock // 7 min trip // Free with wristband. ]

[ 🚗 VEHICLE PARKING ZONE P1 ]

Guarded vehicle parking facility P1 located along Route 38 perimeter. Capacity: 800 motor vehicles with EV fast-charging stations.

[ FEE: 100 CZK (~€4) / 24 hours // Monitored 24/7 by security. ]

[ Protocol 02: Camping, Cashless & Site Infrastructure ]

[ ⛺ TENT VILLAGE CAMPING ZONE ]

Operating window: Friday, July 10 at 12:00 continuously through Monday, July 13 at 12:00.

Includes floodlit perimeter fencing, sanitization stations, flush toilets, hot showers, and fresh potable water refill points.

[ 💳 100% CASHLESS RFID WRISTBANDS ]

The festival operates an entirely cashless environment. All food, beverage, and merchandise purchases are processed via your RFID wristband.

Top-up online before arrival or at 6 on-site terminals using debit/credit card or cash (CZK/EUR). Remaining balance refundable online.

[ 🔒 SECURE LOCKERS & USB-C CHARGING ]

Lockable storage units equipped with internal 20W USB-C PD power sockets situated at the Accreditation Center.

Lockers can be reserved online via festival ticket portal or directly at the Info Point.

[ Protocol 03: Operational FAQ & Regulations ]

[ Q01: WHAT IS THE FESTIVAL INCLEMENT WEATHER & RAIN PROTOCOL? ]

A: Festival of Darkness 2 operates rain or shine. In case of precipitation, Lunar Forest Dome provides a 100% weatherproof, climate-controlled covered shelter with 1,800 capacity. Shows across both stages proceed according to scheduled timetable slots.

[ Q02: ARE CHILDREN AND DOMESTIC PETS ADMITTED? ]

A: Children aged 12 and under receive complimentary admission when accompanied by an adult guardian. Over-ear hearing protection earmuffs are mandatory for minors. Domestic pets are strictly excluded for animal welfare and acoustics safety.

[ Q03: ARE OUTSIDE FOOD AND BEVERAGES PERMITTED? ]

A: Attendees may bring empty reusable plastic or stainless steel bottles (up to 1.0 liter) for free drinking water refills at designated water stations. Glass containers, metal cans, and outside alcoholic beverages are confiscated at security gates.

[ Q04: WHERE ARE MEDICAL FIRST AID AND LOST & FOUND STATIONS? ]

A: The Red Cross Medical Emergency Tent and the Official Lost & Found office are co-located at the North Gate Entrance, staffed 24 hours per day throughout the festival.
