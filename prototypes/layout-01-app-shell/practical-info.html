[ Practical Information for Visitors ]

[ Complete transit directions, campsite facilities, cashless wristband rules, and visitor FAQ for Kutna Hora 2026. ]

[ 🚆 Transit to Kutna Hora ]

[ By Train from Prague / Brno ]

[ Regular direct train connections operate hourly to Kutna Hora main station (approx. 50 minutes from Prague Main Station). ]

[ Festival Shuttle Bus ]

[ Dedicated zero-emission shuttle buses connect Kutna Hora train station to the main festival gate every 15 minutes, complimentary with valid festival tickets. ]

[ Vehicle Parking P1 ]

[ Official secured parking lot P1 is situated at the town perimeter. Capacity: 800 vehicles, daily fee: 100 CZK (~€4). ]

[ Accommodations & Grounds Amenities ]

[ Tent Village (Camping) ]

[ Opens Friday July 10 at 12:00 and operates continuously until Monday July 13 at 12:00. Fenced perimeter with 24/7 security, flush toilets, and warm showers. ]

[ Cashless RFID Payments ]

[ 100% cashless festival experience via high-speed RFID wristbands. Top-up anytime via online portal or physical recharge desks with cash or payment card. ]

[ Lockers & Device Charging ]

[ Lockable personal deposit boxes with dedicated USB-C fast charging sockets located near the accreditation hub. ]

[ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ]

Q: Are children and pets permitted inside the festival grounds?

A: Children aged 12 and under receive complimentary entry accompanied by an adult legal guardian. Pets and domestic animals are strictly prohibited for safety and hearing protection reasons.

Q: What happens if it rains heavily during festival days?

A: The festival proceeds rain or shine. In case of inclement weather, the massive covered Lunar Forest Dome provides 100% rain shelter with non-stop electronic performances.

Q: Can I re-enter the grounds once checked in?

A: Yes. Your fabric festival RFID wristband allows unrestricted re-entry throughout all 3 days.
