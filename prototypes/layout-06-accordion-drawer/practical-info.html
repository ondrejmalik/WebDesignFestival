🚆 [ 1. Getting to Kutná Hora (Train, Bus & Highway Directions) ] Transit
▼

[ Transportation Options from Prague & Beyond ]

[ By Direct Train (Recommended) ]

Departure: Praha hlavní nádraží (Prague Main Station)

Arrival: Kutná Hora hlavní nádraží

Travel Time: Approximately 55 minutes

Frequency: Departs hourly (R9 fast trains and local connections). Integrated into PID (Prague Integrated Transport) tariff zone 7.

[ By Express Bus ]

Departure: Prague Háje (Metro C terminal)

Arrival: Kutná Hora Autobusové nádraží

Travel Time: Approximately 70 minutes

Lines: Regional bus line 381 runs throughout the day.

[ By Car & Highway ]

Route: Via D11 highway to Exit 39 (Poděbrady South), then Route 38 directly to Kutná Hora (approx. 72 km from Prague).

Parking: Official festival park-and-ride lot (P1) located 400m from the main entrance. Parking pass is 200 CZK / weekend.

⛺ [ 3. Tent City, Camping Rules & Hygiene Facilities ] Camp Area
▼

[ Festival Camping & Tent Village Guidelines ]

The secured campsite is located adjacent to the festival perimeter on flat grassy grounds, shaded by mature trees.

[ Camping Check-In & Hours ]

Opens: Thursday, July 9 at 12:00

Closes: Monday, July 13 at 14:00

24-hour reception, perimeter security fence, and wristband checkpoint.

[ Showers & Sanitary Facilities ]

Clean flushing toilets and hot-water shower containers available 24/7.

Free drinking water refill stations located throughout the campsite.

[ Camp Regulations ]

• No open campfires or gas grills (designated cooking zone provided).

• Quiet zone enforced in section C from 03:00 to 10:00.

• No glass containers allowed in the tent village.

💳 [ 4. 100% Cashless RFID System & Refund Policy ] NFC Wristband
▼

[ Fast & Secure On-Site Transactions ]

Festival of Darkness 2 operates a completely cashless environment. Your festival fabric wristband contains an encrypted waterproof NFC microchip used for all payments across food stalls, bars, merch, and lockers.

[ How to Top Up ]

Online Pre-Loading: Top up via our online portal before arrival without transaction fees.

On-Site Stations: Dedicated top-up points accept debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and cash (CZK, EUR).

[ Checking Your Balance ]

Every vendor displays your remaining credit on the terminal after each transaction. Free NFC balance scanners are located near the info point.

[ 100% Balance Refund Guarantee ]

Unspent balances can be refunded back to your bank account online for up to 14 days after the festival with zero administration deduction fee.

❓ [ 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ] 8 Common Queries
▼
[ Q: What happens if it rains heavily during the festival? ] ▼

A: All attendees can immediately transition into the Lunar Forest Dome, a 100% weatherproof covered geodesic dome with a capacity of 1,800 people and non-stop audiovisual programming. The open-air Solar Stage has covered roof structures for artists and equipment and continues unless severe lightning mandates a pause.

[ Q: Are tickets available on the festival gate? ] ▼

A: Unless sold out in advance, a limited contingent of 3-day and single-day tickets will be sold at the accreditation box office. Cash and cards accepted.

[ Q: Can I leave the festival area and re-enter? ] ▼

A: Yes, your fabric festival wristband allows unlimited re-entry throughout the entire weekend. Wristbands that show signs of tampering or damage will be confiscated.

[ Q: Is there luggage storage and phone charging on site? ] ▼

A: Yes, the Safe Box container offers lockable compartments with built-in 230V and USB charging ports. Pre-booking online is recommended.

[ Q: Are dogs or pets allowed on festival grounds? ] ▼

A: Due to extreme sound pressure levels and large crowds, animals are strictly prohibited on festival grounds and in the campsite, with the exception of certified service guide dogs.
