[ The Intersection of Sound, Shadow, and Architecture ]

Festival of Darkness 2 returns to Kutna Hora for its second biennial edition. Across three immersive days (July 10–12, 2026), 60 international and Czech artists converge across two contrasting staging concepts: the open-air Solar Horizon Stage and the weatherproof indoor Lunar Forest Dome.

Our curation bridges meditative modular ambient, crushing dark techno, shoegaze, ethereal dream pop, and visceral industrial noise. Attendees experience both the expansive summer skies and intimate audiovisual projection spaces.

[ Location & Atmosphere ]

Historical industrial periphery of Kutna Hora, 55 minutes by direct express train from Prague Main Station.

[ Sound & Acoustics ]

Custom-tuned d&b audiotechnik line arrays on Solar Horizon Stage; 360-degree immersive surround sound in Lunar Forest Dome.
