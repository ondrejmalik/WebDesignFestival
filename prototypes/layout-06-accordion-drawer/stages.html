[ Solar Horizon Stage — Technical & Acoustic Profile ]

The primary open-air amphitheater nestled into the grassy slopes overlooking the industrial periphery of Kutna Hora. Optimized for major live ensemble bands, driving post-rock crescendos, indie rock, and energetic headline dance sets.

[ Capacity & Atmosphere ]

Max Capacity: 4,500 attendees

Environment: Open-air, tiered amphitheater seating and wide standing lawn.

Operating Hours: 14:00 – 04:00 daily

[ Sound & Lighting Systems ]

PA System: d&b audiotechnik KSL line array system with cardioid subwoofers.

Lighting & Visuals: High-output Martin MAC Viper moving heads, ultra-wide backdrop LED matrix (18x6 meters).

[ Programming Focus ]

Post-Rock, Dream Pop, Shoegaze, Synthwave, Indie Rock, and late-night headliner live electronica.

[ Lunar Forest Dome — Covered Rain Shelter & Immersive Electronics ]

A massive 28-meter geodesic dome fully enclosed with heavy-duty acoustic membrane. Functions as the festival's dedicated indoor sanctuary and guaranteed rain shelter during severe weather, providing continuous uninterrupted audiovisual programming.

[ Capacity & Weatherproof Architecture ]

Max Capacity: 1,800 attendees

Shelter Status: 100% waterproof heavy-gauge cover with raised wooden modular flooring and dry perimeter seating.

Operating Hours: 13:00 – 04:00 daily (Non-stop in rain emergencies)

[ Immersive Audiovisual Specs ]

Spatial Audio: L-Acoustics L-ISA 18.1 multi-channel immersive surround sound system.

Visual Mapping: 360-degree dome projection mapping powered by eight 4K laser projectors.

[ Weather Emergency & Shelter Protocol ]

In the event of summer storms or heavy rainfall, all attendees on open grounds are invited into the Lunar Forest Dome. The venue features multiple high-capacity entrance baffles and emergency exits.

[ Accessibility (ZTP / PRM) ]

All paths between the Main Gate, Solar Stage, and Lunar Dome are paved and step-free. Raised wheelchair viewing platforms are installed at both stages with unobstructed sightlines.
