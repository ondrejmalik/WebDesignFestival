[ Stages & Venue Architecture ]

[ Festival of Darkness 2 takes place in the historic surroundings of Kutna Hora across two acoustically engineered stages. ]

[ 🌧️ Lunar Forest Dome (Indoor Covered Rain Shelter) ]

[ COVERED DOME • CAPACITY: 1,800 • INDOOR AUDIENCE ]

[ Architectural Design: ] A geodesic dome situated in the southern woodland zone of the festival grounds. Outfitted with heavy weatherproof acoustic membrane, full overhead rain shielding, and 360-degree panoramic projection mapping.

[ Musical Focus: ] Dark techno, hypnotic ambient, modular synthesizers, glitch, and immersive generative electronics.

[ Inclement Weather Sanctuary: ] In case of rain showers or summer thunderstorms, Lunar Forest Dome serves as the primary dry retreat on site, operating continuous uninterrupted soundscapes.

[ ☀️ Solar Horizon Stage (Main Open-Air Stage) ]

[ MAIN OUTDOOR STAGE • CAPACITY: 4,500 • NATURAL AMPHITHEATER ]

[ Architectural Design: ] A large-scale open-air stage nestled in the central natural meadow. Features extended line-array sound towers, expansive scenic lighting rigs, and sloping lawn viewing.

[ Musical Focus: ] Post-rock headliners, dream pop, ethereal shoegaze, indie rock quintets, and dynamic evening climaxes.

[ Weather Guidelines: ] Operates in all standard weather conditions. Ponchos and rain capes are available at official info booths.
