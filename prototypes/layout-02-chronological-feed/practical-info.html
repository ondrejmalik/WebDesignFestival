[ 🚆 Transport & Getting to Kutna Hora ]

[ By Train from Prague ]

[ Direct R-line and Sp-line trains depart hourly from Praha Hlavni Nadrazi directly to Kutna Hora hl.n. Travel duration is approximately 50 minutes. ]

[ Return express trains operate throughout the night following the final performances. ]

[ ⛺ Camping & Accommodation ]

[ Camping Grounds A & B ]

[ • Camping opens Thursday, July 9 at 16:00 and closes Monday, July 13 at 12:00. ]

[ • Camping Zone A is designated as a Quiet / Resting Zone after 01:00. ]

[ • Clean drinking water stations, flush toilets, and hot shower containers are provided free of charge. ]

[ • Open campfires, gas stoves, and glass bottles are strictly prohibited for safety reasons. ]

[ 💳 Cashless RFID Wristband System ]

[ • All food stalls, beverage bars, and festival merch operate exclusively with RFID wristband payments. ]

[ • Top-up your wristband via debit/credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or cash at on-site top-up booths. ]

[ • Leftover credit can be refunded online up to 14 days following the festival with zero processing fees. ]

[ ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ]

[ Q: What happens if it rains heavily during the festival? ]

[ A: Our indoor stage, Lunar Forest Dome, is an engineered covered sanctuary with 1,800 capacity. Shows on this stage run continuously regardless of weather. Solar Horizon Stage features covered stage rigging and will continue unless severe storm warnings are issued. ]

[ Q: Are ear protection and noise reduction earplugs available? ]

[ A: Yes, free foam earplugs and reusable high-fidelity acoustic filters are available at all info points and merch stands. ]

[ Q: Is the festival site wheelchair accessible? ]

[ A: Both Solar Horizon Stage and Lunar Forest Dome have dedicated viewing platforms, paved approach pathways, and accessible sanitation facilities. ]
