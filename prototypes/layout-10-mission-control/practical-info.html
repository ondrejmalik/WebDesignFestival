[ 🚆 Transportation to Kutna Hora ]

[ Regional Train from Prague ]

Hourly direct trains depart from Prague Main Station (Hlavní Nádraží) to Kutná Hora hl.n. (52 minutes travel time).

[ Ground Shuttle Fleet ]

Festival shuttle buses cycle between Kutná Hora main railway station and the festival main gate every 15 minutes during peak hours (11:00–04:00).

[ ⛺ Camping Village & Cashless Ground Control ]

[ Tent Village ]

Open from Thursday July 9, 12:00 until Monday July 13, 14:00. Equipped with 24/7 warm showers, mobile restrooms, device charging hubs, and secure lockers.

[ NFC Cashless System ]

All festival vendors operate via NFC wristband. Top up online before the event or at physical top-up kiosks across the grounds.
