[ ☀️ Solar Horizon Stage ]

[ Wireframe Diagram: Open-Air Amphitheater Stage ]

Type: Open-air natural amphitheater with panoramic hill slope seating.

Capacity: 4,500 visitors

Programming Focus: Daylight to midnight live band performances, post-rock, indie rock, and alternative headliners.

Acoustics: Expansive line-array system tailored for organic instrument reverberation.

[ 🌙 Lunar Forest Dome (Covered Dome – Scenario 1) ]

[ ALL-WEATHER RAIN SHELTER ]
[ Wireframe Diagram: 360-Degree Covered Dome Projection Rig ]

Type: Heavy-duty covered marquee dome providing 100% waterproof rain shelter.

Capacity: 1,800 visitors

Programming Focus: 360-degree laser mapping, dark techno, modular ambient, synthpop, and experimental audiovisual club sets.

Inclement Weather Protocol: In case of heavy rainfall, programming continues uninterrupted under the covered dome.
