[ The Kutna Hora festival grounds feature 2 main musical stages offering completely distinct sensory atmospheres. ]

[ ☀️ Solar Horizon Stage ]

Stage Type: Natural open-air amphitheater

Capacity: 4,500 visitors

Focus: Afternoon and evening live band performances (Post-Rock, Indie Rock, live audiovisual sets).

Location: Western festival amphitheater, Sedlec Grounds

[ ☀️ View Solar Horizon Stage Schedule → ]

[ 🌙 Lunar Forest Dome (Covered Dome) ]

Stage Type: Large-capacity covered dome with complete all-weather rain shelter

Capacity: 1,800 visitors

Focus: Late-night club sets, dark techno, modular ambient, experimental electronics, and 360° projections.

Ideal refuge when it rains – music runs continuously under a solid covered roof!

Location: Eastern forest sector, Weatherproof Dome
