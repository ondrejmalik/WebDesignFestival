About this event

⏱ 3 days of continuous music
🎟 Mobile e-ticket & wristband entry

Festival of Darkness 2 returns to Kutná Hora from 10 to 12 July 2026 with a curated program spanning post-rock, dream pop, indie electronic, modular ambient, and late-night techno.

Explore live bands under the open sky at Solar Horizon Stage or step inside the 360-degree audiovisual projections of Lunar Forest Dome.

Two distinct stages

2 stages
Open-air main stage Capacity: 4,500

Lunar Forest Dome
