What is "Free Training Mode"?

"Free Training Mode" is a specific Deadly Assault stage available in beta releases, used as a neutral testing ground for Agents. The enemy utilized is a modified version of Dead End Butcher, featuring an increased HP pool of 3,347,325,000, zero Resistances or Weaknesses, and no Stage Buffs.
