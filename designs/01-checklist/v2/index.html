Stages

Two contrasting environments designed for open-air live bands and immersive dome electronics.

Solar Horizon Stage

Central open-air amphitheater for major band performances, indie rock, and dance headliners.

  • Indie rock, post-rock, synthpop, and orchestral electronic headliners
  • Daily sets from 13:00 until 02:00

Lunar Forest Dome

Atmospheric covered indoor stage focusing on techno, ambient, experimental electronics, and 360-degree light projections.

  • 360-degree dome projection mapping and spatial sound
  • Modular ambient, dub techno, industrial, and acid live sets
  • Late-night sessions running until 04:00
