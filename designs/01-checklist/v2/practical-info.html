Venue & Transport

Festival grounds are located on the northern edge of Kutná Hora, 1.8 km from the main railway station.

Rail & Shuttle Recommended arrival

Train from Prague & Brno

Direct České dráhy fast trains (R9 Vysočina) run hourly from Praha hlavní nádraží to Kutná Hora hl.n. in 50 minutes.

  • Dedicated festival shuttle buses depart Kutná Hora hl.n. every 20 minutes (10:00-02:30)
  • Night relief trains toward Prague depart at 02:35 and 04:15 on Saturday and Sunday
  • 25-minute signposted pedestrian path from the station via Sedlec
Car & Bike Pre-booked passes

Parking & Drop-Off

Follow temporary yellow festival signage from Road I/38 toward North Meadow Parking (P1 & P2), 400 m from check-in.

  • Weekend parking passes must be reserved online prior to arrival; no overnight sleeping in cars
  • Free guarded bicycle rack at the West Gate with basic repair tools and pump
  • Taxi and rideshare Kiss & Ride zone located 150 m south of the main box office

Camping & Accommodation

On-site tent meadows open Thursday 9 July at 16:00 and close Monday 13 July at 12:00.

Zone A Standard Meadow

General Tent Camp

Included with Camping Pass · 3 min walk to gates

Shaded grass pitches with 24-hour perimeter lighting, free drinking water stations, and staffed luggage lockers.

  • Hot showers and ceramic restrooms cleaned continuously
  • Morning coffee kiosk and phone charging hub
Zone B Quiet & Pre-Pitched

Silent Camp & Bell Tents

23:00-08:00 strict quiet hours

Acoustic-buffered meadow for uninterrupted rest, plus ready-pitched 2-person and 4-person canvas bell tents with mattresses.

  • Dedicated entrance lane and private sanitary block
  • Bedding, solar lantern, and padlock included in pre-pitched packages
Town Kutná Hora Center

Hotels & Guesthouses

2.2 km from Solar Horizon Stage

Historic center hotels, pensions, and student dormitories connect directly via the Night Line city shuttle.

  • Unlimited re-entry with valid 3-day festival wristband
  • Partner accommodation desk at the Main Box Office

Cashless & Food

All bars, food trucks, and merchandise stalls operate exclusively via NFC festival wristbands.

NFC System CZK & EUR accepted

Wristband Top-Up & Refunds

Link your ticket online in advance or top up at six yellow Top-Up Stations across the grounds using contactless card or cash.

  • Zero activation fee; remaining balance above 50 CZK refunded automatically within 5 business days
  • Instant balance check displayed at every terminal after each tap
  • Online top-up receipts stored in your festival account
Catering 12:00-03:30 daily

Food Court & Hydration

28 regional food vendors between Solar Horizon Stage and Lunar Forest Dome, plus a late-night hot food alley beside the dome.

  • Full vegan, vegetarian, and certified gluten-free options clearly marked at every stall
  • Free potable water refill points at both stages (empty soft flasks up to 0.75 L permitted)
  • Returnable hard-cup deposit system (50 CZK) across all bars

Accessibility

Step-free routing and dedicated support facilities for visitors with disabilities or sensory sensitivities.

Mobility ZTP / ZTP-P

Step-Free Access & Viewing Platforms

Compacted gravel pathways and modular trackway mats link the Accessible Entrance, both stages, restrooms, and the food court.

  • Elevated wheelchair viewing platform at Solar Horizon Stage with companion seating
  • Level ground-floor entry and reserved front-side zone inside Lunar Forest Dome
  • ZTP/P cardholders receive one complimentary companion pass upon registration
Sensory & Care Info Point West

Low-Sensory Room & Medical Storage

Quiet decompression space and refrigerated medication storage are available throughout all festival operating hours.

  • Strobe and high-intensity visual warnings posted outside Lunar Forest Dome
  • Complimentary foam earplugs at Info Points; noise-reducing defenders available on deposit
  • Wheelchair battery charging and refrigerated insulin storage at the Medical Hub
