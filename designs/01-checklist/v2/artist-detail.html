Biography

Formed in Paris in 2019, Starlight Odyssey bridges French electro-pop craftsmanship with driving post-rock dynamics. Their live show centers on hardware sequencers, vocoder harmonies, and custom reactive stage lighting built specifically for open-air night slots.

  • Full live hardware and multi-instrumentalist stage setup
  • Extended 75-minute festival headline arrangement (23:30–00:45)
  • Synchronized open-air light choreography on Solar Horizon Stage
