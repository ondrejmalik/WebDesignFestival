Venue & Transport

Kutná Hora — Sedlec / Vrchlice Grounds
Rail & Shuttle 55 min from Praha hl.n.

Getting There by Train

Direct ČD fast trains (R9 / Ex) run hourly from Prague Main Station (Praha hl.n.) to Kutná Hora hl.n. Dedicated festival shuttle buses depart every 20 minutes from the station forecourt directly to the main gate (8 min ride, free with wristband).

Parking & Drop-Off GPS: 49.9584° N, 15.2862° E

Arriving by Car or Bike

Follow D11 (Exit 42) or Route 38 toward Kutná Hora and follow orange festival signage to P1 North Meadow Parking (400 CZK / weekend). A guarded bicycle rack is available free of charge right outside the main accreditation tent.

Cashless Payments

All bars, food trucks, and merch booths operate cashless via your festival NFC wristband (or contactless bank card / Apple Pay / Google Pay). Top up online before arrival or at 6 station kiosks with CZK/EUR cash or card. Unused balance is refunded automatically within 5 business days.

Street Food & Bars Returnable Cups (50 CZK)

Dining, Dietary Options & Water

Over 25 Czech and international food stalls serve specialty coffee, wood-fired pizza, Asian street food, and classic grill dishes, with clearly labeled vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free menus. Free potable water refill points are located beside both stages.

Medical & Care

24/7 Paramedics & Safe Space Patrol

Two staffed medical tents (Main Arena North & Camp Gate) and roaming harm-reduction / awareness teams operate continuously. Emergency line on site: +420 222 112 026.

Non-Stop
Gate Policy

What Can I Bring Inside the Arena?

Allowed: empty reusable plastic/metal water bottles (up to 0.75 L), small backpacks, compact cameras, raincoats, and medication. Prohibited: glass bottles, outside alcohol, drones, laser pointers, and fireworks.

Lockers at Gate
Age & ID

Entry Requirements & Re-Entry

Visitors aged 15+ may enter independently with valid photo ID; under-15s must be accompanied by a legal guardian. Unlimited re-entry is included once your NFC wristband is activated.
