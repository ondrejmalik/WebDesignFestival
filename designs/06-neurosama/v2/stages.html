Solar Horizon Stage

Capacity: 4,500
Open-air main stage 30 Sets / 3 Days

Open-Air Central Amphitheater

Central open-air amphitheater for major band performances, indie rock, and dance headliners.

Set within the natural basin of the Kutná Hora grounds, Solar Horizon pairs wide sightlines with golden-hour live instrumentation that transitions into high-energy electronic and indie-dance headliners after sundown.

Lunar Forest Dome

Capacity: 1,800
Covered audiovisual dome 30 Sets / 3 Days

360° Projection & Club Sanctuary

Atmospheric covered indoor stage focusing on techno, ambient, experimental electronics, and 360-degree light projections.

Sheltered beneath a geodesic acoustic canopy at the edge of the woodland, the dome envelops dancers in spatial surround sound and real-time generative visuals running until dawn.
