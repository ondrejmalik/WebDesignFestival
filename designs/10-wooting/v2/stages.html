Solar Horizon Stage

Central open-air amphitheater for major band performances, indie rock, and dance headliners.

Designed with high-output line array towers and expansive open-air acoustics, the Solar Horizon Stage delivers massive headroom and crystal-clear dynamics across the entire natural amphitheater bowl. Ideal for sunset performances and peak-time festival energy.

Operating window 13:00 - 02:00
Acoustic focus Indie Rock, Synthpop, Electronic Live

Lunar Forest Dome

Atmospheric covered indoor stage focusing on techno, ambient, experimental electronics, and 360-degree light projections.

A fully enclosed 360-degree projection dome featuring spatial discrete multi-channel surround sound and immersive laser architecture. Built for deep hypnotic sets, raw modular hardware techno, and late-night nocturnal immersion.

Operating window 14:00 - 04:00
Acoustic focus Techno, Modular Ambient, IDM
