Venue & Transport

Located in the Sedlec grounds on the eastern edge of Kutná Hora, 65 km east of Prague.

Rail & Shuttle 55 min from Praha hl.n.

Train to Kutná Hora hl.n.

Direct České dráhy fast trains (R9 / Rx) depart Prague Main Station every hour. Dedicated festival shuttle buses run every 20 minutes between Kutná Hora hl.n. and Main Gate North from Friday 11:30 until Monday 11:00.

Shuttle: 50 CZK / trip 1.8 km walk via marked path
Car & Bicycle D11 / Route 38

Parking & Drop-Off

Follow festival signage from Route 38 to P1 Day Parking and P2 Weekend Camper Lot (500 m from check-in). Secure covered bicycle racks with free repair tools are available right next to the main entrance.

Weekend Pass: 350 CZK No sleeping in P1 cars

Cashless & Food

All bars, food trucks, and merch booths operate 100% cashless via NFC festival wristbands or contactless bank cards.

Payments & Refunds

Link your ticket online in advance or top up at six yellow kiosks on site using card or cash. Unused wristband balances over 50 CZK are refunded automatically within 5 business days after the festival.

Apple Pay & Google Pay accepted Zero activation fee
22 Vendors 12:00 - 03:30

Food Village & Free Water

Curated Czech and international street food with dedicated vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free kitchens. Free drinking water refill stations are located beside both stages and every sanitary block.

Returnable cups: 50 CZK deposit Empty soft flasks allowed
