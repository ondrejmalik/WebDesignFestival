Atmosphere & Sightlines

Set in a natural grass-terraced basin facing west, Solar Horizon captures golden-hour daylight before transitioning into open-sky night lighting. Wide lateral sightlines and terraced standing tiers ensure unobstructed views across the entire 4,500-person amphitheater.

Sound & Stage Architecture

Flown dual-hang line-array PA with cardioid sub-bass alignment minimizes spill toward the nearby woodland perimeter. Built with a 16m × 12m covered performance deck, quick-change rolling risers, and horizon-wide kinetic pixel bars synced to live instrumentation.

Lunar Forest Dome

Atmospheric covered indoor stage focusing on techno, ambient, experimental electronics, and 360-degree light projections.

Stage schedule

Atmosphere & Immersion

Sheltered beneath a 34-meter blackout geodesic structure tucked into the wooded edge of the grounds, Lunar Forest Dome operates as a weather-sealed nocturnal sanctuary. Low-lying haze, perimeter chill alcoves, and an in-the-round central booth draw dancers inside the projection canopy.

Projection & Sound Spec

Eight edge-blended laser projectors map real-time generative visuals across the full overhead hemisphere, paired with addressable LED ribs and an 8-point surround sound array tuned for sub-30 Hz tactile clarity during modular live sets and late-night techno sessions.
