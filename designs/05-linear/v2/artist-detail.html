Nordic widescreen electropop

Formed in Copenhagen, Starlight Runners bridge crisp Scandinavian synthpop production with anthemic live instrumentation. Combining modular polyphonic synthesizers, dual vocals, and driving live drums, their arrangements build from intimate pulse patterns into luminous late-evening crescendos.
