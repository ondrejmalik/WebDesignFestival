Artist Biography

⚡ Full Live Band & Custom Light Rig
🌙 Sunday Night Main Stage Headliner

Formed in Copenhagen in 2019, Starlight Runners quickly rose across the European festival circuit with a sound that pairs shimmering Scandinavian melodies with nocturnal club momentum. Built around twin vintage polysynths, live drums, and soaring lead vocals, their sets balance intimate emotional weight with hands-in-the-air festival scale.

At Festival of Darkness 2, Starlight Runners headline the final night on the open-air Solar Horizon Stage right between UK dance-punk outfit Prismatics and Prague orchestral electronic closer Final Cascade, debuting new material alongside their breakthrough anthems.
