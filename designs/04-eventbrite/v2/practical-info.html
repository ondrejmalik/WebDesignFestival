1. Venue & Transport

Kutná Hora, CZ

The Kutná Hora Festival Grounds sit on the green edge of town, 2.2 km from Kutná Hora hlavní nádraží (main railway station) and 1.5 km from St. Barbara's Cathedral.

🚆

By Train (ČD / RegioJet)

Hourly direct R9 fast trains from Praha hlavní nádraží reach Kutná Hora hl.n. in 48 minutes. Dedicated festival shuttle buses run every 20 minutes from the station forecourt (Fri 10:30 – Sun 03:30, 50 CZK single ride).

🚗

By Car & Parking

Take D11 Exit 42 toward Kolín / Kutná Hora via Road I/38. Secured P1 Festival Parking is 400 m from the main gate (350 CZK weekend pass; pre-booking recommended).

🚌

Night Return Shuttles

Late-night shuttles depart Main Gate East at 01:30, 02:30, and 03:30 each night directly to Kutná Hora hl.n. and the historic town center.

🚲

Cycling & Drop-Off

Free guarded bike racks operate 24/7 beside Gate North. Taxi and rideshare kiss-and-ride drop-off is located on Masarykova avenue, 150 m from check-in.

2. Camping & Accommodation

Opens Thu 9 Jul, 16:00

Stay right next to Solar Horizon Stage and Lunar Forest Dome in our shaded oak and birch grove, or choose partner hotels across Kutná Hora.

Tent Village Included in Camping Pass

Kutná Hora Grove Camp

Grassy pitches 3 minutes from the arena entrance. Includes 24/7 security, hot showers (07:00–23:00), drinking water points, and a 24-hour phone charging station.

Pre-Pitched & Vans Limited Capacity

Chill Glamping & Camper Zone

Pre-pitched 2- and 4-person bell tents with foam mattresses and solar lanterns, plus a dedicated campervan field with optional 230V electric hookups.

Check-In Thu 16:00 – Sun 12:00

Campground Rules & Quiet Zone

Open fires, gas stoves, and glass bottles are strictly prohibited inside the camp. Family & Quiet Sector B enforces low noise from 01:00 to 08:00.

Town Hotels 1.5 – 3.0 km away

Partner Guesthouses in Kutná Hora

Use code DARKNESS26 when booking at Hotel U Kata, Penzion Barbora, or Boutique Hotel Hubert for late checkout until 13:00 on Monday, July 13.

3. Cashless Wristbands & Food

NFC Wristband System

All bars, food trucks, and official merch stalls operate exclusively via NFC festival wristbands. Cash and bank cards are accepted at top-up kiosks to load credit onto your chip.

⌚

Top-Up & Auto-Refund

Pre-load your wristband online before arrival or top up at 6 yellow NFC booths on site (CZK or EUR). Unspent balances over 50 CZK are refunded automatically to your bank account within 5 business days.

🥗

Food Village & Dietary Options

22 street-food vendors serving Czech craft classics, wood-fired pizza, Asian bowls, and specialty coffee. Over 60% of stalls offer labeled vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free menus.

💧

Free Drinking Water & Cups

Free potable water refill stations are located beside Solar Horizon Stage, outside Lunar Forest Dome, and at the main toilets. Reusable festival cups carry a 50 CZK refundable deposit.

🛍️

Official Merch & Vinyl Store

Located by the central plaza. Stocks limited Festival of Darkness 2 screen-printed tees, artist records, earplugs, and rain ponchos.

4. Accessibility & Inclusion

ZTP / ZTP-P Welcome

Festival of Darkness 2 is committed to step-free access across both stages, sanitary facilities, and viewing areas.

Companion Pass Free Companion

ZTP / ZTP-P Ticket Policy

Holders of a valid ZTP/P card (or international equivalent) receive free entry for one accompanying assistant upon presenting their pass at the Dedicated Accessibility Check-In lane.

Viewing Decks Both Stages

Elevated Wheelchair Platforms

Ramped viewing decks with clear sightlines and power outlets for electric wheelchairs are available at Solar Horizon Stage and inside Lunar Forest Dome.

Sensory Care Quiet Zone

Accessible Restrooms & Low-Sensory Lounge

Euro-key wheelchair toilets are positioned at every sanitary block. A low-stimulation chillout tent near First Aid offers seating, shade, and free foam earplugs.

5. Safety, Permitted Items & FAQ

24/7 Medical & Welfare

Bag checks take place at all gates to keep the arena safe. Strobe and intense 360-degree laser visuals are used inside Lunar Forest Dome after 20:00.

Allowed Inside

What to Bring

Empty soft plastic water bottle (up to 1L), small daypack (max 25L), compact point-and-shoot camera, sunscreen, prescription medication with ID, and rainwear.

Not Permitted

Prohibited Items

Glass bottles, cans, outside alcohol, drones, professional detachable-lens cameras without press accreditation, umbrellas, weapons, fireworks, and pets (except assistance dogs).

Entry & Re-Entry Unlimited with Wristband

Can I leave and re-enter the festival grounds?

Yes. Once your digital e-ticket is scanned and exchanged for a fabric NFC wristband, you may exit and re-enter freely throughout the weekend.

FAQ 01
Age Policy 15+ Solo / Under 15 w/ Adult

Is there a minimum age requirement?

Visitors aged 15 and older may enter independently with valid photo ID. Children under 15 are welcome when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian (under 10 enters free).

FAQ 02
Medical & Harm Reduction 24/7 On-Site

Where do I find medical help or Safe Space support?

Two staffed Red Cross ambulance points and our non-judgmental Safe Space welfare tent operate continuously between Solar Horizon Stage and the Main Gate. Emergency line: +420 800 555 019.

FAQ 03
Lockers & Lost Property Main Plaza

Are lockers and lost-and-found available?

PIN-code lockers with USB-C charging ports can be rented by the day or weekend near Gate South. Lost & Found is hosted at the Info Booth during festival hours and at Kutná Hora Town Info on Monday.
