Solar Horizon Stage

Central open-air amphitheater for major band performances, indie rock, and dance headliners.

Positioned along the western ridge of the Kutná Hora grounds, Solar Horizon pairs wide natural sightlines with sunset-to-midnight programming. Full live bands, electro-pop headliners, and Nu-Disco/Nu-Electro closers perform across a 24-meter proscenium rig engineered for crisp low-end dispersion across the natural slope.

Lunar Forest Dome

Atmospheric covered indoor stage focusing on techno, ambient, experimental electronics, and 360-degree light projections.

Sheltered beneath an acoustically treated geodesic canopy on the wooded eastern edge of the site, Lunar Forest Dome envelops the dancefloor in synchronized 360° laser-mapped visuals and quadraphonic surround stacks. Afternoon modular ambient and field-recording sessions evolve after dusk into hardware techno, acid, and deep dawn closers.

Moving between stages

Both stages sit inside the Kutná Hora Festival Grounds with a 4-minute illuminated forest footpath connecting the open-air amphitheater and the covered dome. All festival wristbands grant unrestricted access to both venues throughout the weekend, subject to real-time dome safety capacity during peak late-night sets.
