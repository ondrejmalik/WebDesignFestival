Located on the wooded edge of Kutná Hora (Sedlec / Kaňk ridge), 70 km east of Prague and 15 minutes from UNESCO heritage landmarks.

Camping & Accommodation

On-site tent fields open Friday, July 10 at 10:00 and close Sunday, July 12 at 15:00.

Standard Camp 450 CZK / weekend

Meadow Tent Zone

Grassy pitches 250 m from Solar Horizon Stage. Includes 24-hour freshwater stations, ceramic toilets, hot showers (07:00–22:00), and free phone charging lockers at Info Tent.

Quiet Forest Camp 650 CZK / weekend

Shaded Pine Grove

Acoustic buffer zone behind Lunar Forest Dome with enforced quiet hours (02:00–09:00), dedicated shower block, and shaded hammock lounge.

Campervan & Car 1,100 CZK / pitch

Caravan & Roof-Tent Lot

Level gravel plots (7 × 5 m) next to Gate B with chemical toilet disposal, potable water refill, and optional 16A CEE power hookup.

Town Hotels 2.5 km away

Kutná Hora Partner Stays

Guesthouses and hotels in Kutná Hora historic center and Sedlec are served by the night shuttle loop running until 04:30 every night.

Cashless Payments & Food

All bars, food trucks, and merchandise stalls operate strictly cashless for fast service between sets.

NFC & Contactless 0 CZK activation

Wristband Chip & Bank Cards

Pay directly with Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or top up your NFC festival wristband online or at four cash/card top-up kiosks. Unused balances over 50 CZK are refunded free within 14 days.

Local & Plant-Based 12:00 – 03:30

Food Court & Free Water

18 street-food vendors offering Czech craft specialties, vegan/vegetarian bowls, and gluten-free options. Free drinking-water refill taps are located beside both stages; reusable returnable cups carry a 50 CZK deposit.

Accessibility (ZTP / ZTP/P)

Step-free routes connect the accessible entrance, both stages, sanitation facilities, and the shuttle drop-off.

Safety, Entry Rules & FAQ

Full 3-day timetable →
24/7 Care Emergency 112

Medical, Harm Reduction & Awareness

The staffed medical station and Safe Space awareness tent sit midway between Solar Horizon Stage and Lunar Forest Dome. Trained medics and harm-reduction counselors are on duty around the clock.

Gate Check Max bag 40 × 30 × 20 cm

Permitted & Prohibited Items

Allowed: empty reusable plastic/aluminum bottles up to 0.75 L, sunscreen, rain ponchos, compact cameras. Prohibited: glass containers, outside alcohol, open flames, drones, and professional audio/video rigs without press accreditation.

Re-Entry & Age 18+ after 22:00

Wristbands & Age Policy

3-day wristbands allow unlimited re-entry through Gate A. Visitors under 15 must be accompanied by a legal guardian; after 22:00, Lunar Forest Dome is restricted to guests aged 18 and over.

Lockers & Lost/Found Gate A Info Desk

Storage & Weather Contingency

PIN-code lockers (150 CZK/day) are available inside the main entrance. Performances continue in light rain; in severe weather alerts, pause instructions are broadcast on stage screens and PA systems.
