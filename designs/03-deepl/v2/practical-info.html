Venue & Transport

Located on the edge of historic Kutná Hora, 65 km east of Prague with direct rail links and dedicated night shuttles.

Arrival & Gate Times GPS: 49.9484° N, 15.2682° E
10-12 July 2026
By Train (Recommended)
Prague hl.n. → Kutná Hora hl.n.

Hourly direct R9 and Os trains take 50-65 minutes. Free festival shuttle buses run every 20 minutes from Kutná Hora hlavní nádraží to the main gate.

By Car & Night Shuttles
D11 Exit 42 / Route 38

Follow festival signs to P1/P2 meadow parking (350 CZK for the whole weekend). Night return shuttles depart for Prague Černý Most at 02:30 and 04:15.

Wristband exchange opens Friday 10 July at 12:00 (2 hours before first set) Inspect stage layout

Camping & Accommodation

Two guarded camping zones sit directly adjacent to the festival grounds, with 24-hour reception and hot showers.

Standard & Quiet Camp Opens Fri 10:00 - Mon 12:00

Meadow Tent Village

Flat grassy pitches divided into General Camp and Silent Family/Rest Camp. Includes drinking water stations, flushable toilets, hot showers (token-free), and a 24/7 phone charging desk.

5 min walk to Solar Horizon Stage Tent pass required per person
Pre-Pitched & Town Hotels Check-in from Fri 12:00

Glamping & Kutná Hora Stays

Pre-erected cotton bell tents with foam mattresses, solar lanterns, and lockable storage lockers. Prefer solid walls? Partner guesthouses in Kutná Hora old town are 10 minutes away by shuttle.

Lockers & cloakroom at main gate Valid 3-day festival pass required

Accessibility

Step-free routing and dedicated support facilities across both stages, camping, and transport.

Viewing & Grounds ZTP / ZTP-P Companion Free

Step-Free Access & Platforms

Hardened matting paths connect the accessible entrance, accessible restrooms, food court, and raised viewing platforms at both Solar Horizon Stage and Lunar Forest Dome. Holders of ZTP/P cards receive one complimentary companion ticket.

Reserved parking 80 m from gate Assistance dogs welcome
Sensory & Medical Quiet Zone Available

Low-Sensory Area & Lighting Notice

Lunar Forest Dome uses 360-degree strobe and laser projections throughout evening sets. A shaded low-sensory rest tent with free foam earplugs, chilled storage for temperature-sensitive medication, and wheelchair charging points operates near Info Point.

Strobe warnings posted at Dome entry 24/7 medical tent on site
