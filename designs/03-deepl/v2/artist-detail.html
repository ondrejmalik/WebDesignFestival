Starlight Runners

Copenhagen-based electronic trio blending analog arpeggiators, widescreen percussion, and kinetic live vocals for the Sunday night headline slot on Solar Horizon Stage.

Artist Profile & Live Set

Electropop Headliner · Origin: Denmark (DK) · Performer ID: perf-49

Formed in Copenhagen in 2019, Starlight Runners built their reputation across Northern European open-air festivals by replacing pre-sequenced backing tracks with live modular synthesizers, dual drum pads, and soaring harmonic vocals.

For their Festival of Darkness 2 appearance in Kutná Hora, the group presents an extended 80-minute closing-night production tailored for the Solar Horizon open-air amphitheater, pairing tracks from their acclaimed LP Northern Pulse with unreleased nocturnal club edits.
